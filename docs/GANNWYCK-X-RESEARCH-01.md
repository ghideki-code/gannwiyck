# GannWyck X Research Memo 01

Date: 2026-09-20
Scope: public posts attributed to @gannwyck, cross-checked against the public GannWyck site.
Purpose: extract testable mechanics without changing the frozen V5.6 model.

## Source-derived observations

1. Model 1 sequencing
A public @gannwyck post on PEPE describes: "1st Tap confirmed", "2nd Tap in the making", and "3rd Tap will come after the push up". This supports treating Tap 3 as a later structural event, not as an immediate third touch.

2. Distribution objective
Recent BTC posts explicitly pair "Range low still open" with "GannWyck Model 1 Distribution". This is evidence that an unresolved range boundary can be treated as an active objective in the author's reading.

3. Multi-timeframe context
Recent BTC and ETH posts describe Model 1 simultaneously across HTF/MTF contexts. ETH is described with ETH, ETH.D and ETH/BTC all printing Model 1 Accumulation.

4. Gann time confluence
Recent BTC posts explicitly state that the Gann clock lined up with Model 1, citing 180 Gann Day for a major swing high and 360 Gann Day for a major swing low.

5. Other context layers
The public site places Market Structure/BOS, Supply & Demand, Ranges, Liquidity, and Model 1 as successive parts of the method. It also describes backtesting and forward testing as separate validation phases.

## What this does NOT establish

- It does not define the exact mathematical construction of the Gann clock.
- It does not establish that 180/360-day timing has predictive power.
- It does not define exact Tap-distance thresholds.
- It does not prove a specific DL/Fibonacci anchor.
- It does not establish that every public post was published before the relevant market move.
- It does not justify changing the frozen V5.6 model.

## Testable hypotheses for a separate research branch

H1: Tap 3 should require a meaningful temporal/price displacement from Tap 2 rather than merely a minimum two-bar gap.

H2: An unresolved range boundary may be useful as a target-state variable for Distribution/Accumulation, but only if defined without future information.

H3: HTF Model 1 context may act as a regime filter for lower-timeframe Model 1 signals.

H4: Gann time-cycle confluence can be tested as an independent feature, starting with 180 and 360 calendar-day distances from confirmed structural pivots.

H5: Multi-asset confirmation (for example ETH + ETH.D + ETH/BTC) can be represented as contextual evidence, but must be tested out-of-sample and not treated as proof from anecdotal posts.

## Research rule

Do not modify frozen V5.6. Any implementation from this memo must be isolated in a research version, evaluated on chronological train/validation/OOS splits, and rejected if the added feature does not improve robustness after costs and multiple-testing controls.
